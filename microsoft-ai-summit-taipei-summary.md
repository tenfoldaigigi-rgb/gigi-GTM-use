## 3. In-Demand Skills for the New Era of Work

**Source:** LinkedIn Work Change Report 2025

Top skills for 2025:

1. **AI literacy** - categorized as an *AI capability*
2. **Conflict mitigation** - categorized as a *Human strength*
3. **Adaptability** - Human strength
4. **Process optimization** - Human strength
5. **Innovative thinking** - Human strength

**Key insight:** While AI literacy tops the list, 4 out of 5 top skills are uniquely human strengths - reinforcing that the future of work is human + AI, not AI replacing humans.

## 4. The Journey to Becoming a "Frontier Firm"

Every company progresses through three phases of AI adoption:

| Phase | Model | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| **Phase 1** | Human with assistant | Every employee has an AI assistant that helps them work better and faster |
| **Phase 2** | Human-agent teams | Agents join teams as "digital colleagues," taking on specific tasks at human direction |
| **Phase 3** | Human-led, agent operated | Humans set direction and agents run entire business processes and workflows, checking in as needed |

Value is measured and realized differently for employees vs. the organization at each phase.

## 9. Updating External Services FAQs

**Demo: AI agent for automated FAQ management**

This slide illustrates an agentic workflow for maintaining customer-facing FAQ content:

### The Workflow:
1. **Understand the new service** — Agent ingests details about new/updated services
2. **List FAQ questions about it** — Agent generates relevant Q&A pairs
3. **Create text response to answer** — Agent drafts comprehensive answers
4. **Send to human to allow edits** — Human reviews and refines the draft
5. **Monitor usage data for revised FAQs** — Track how customers interact with updated content

### Key Elements:
- **Agent can build/update FAQs** automatically based on service changes
- **Human does final review and corrections** — maintaining quality control
- The process improves over time by monitoring real usage data

**Key takeaway:** This is a concrete Phase 2/3 example where an AI agent handles the bulk of repetitive content work while humans maintain editorial oversight — a "human-in-the-loop" pattern that balances efficiency with accuracy.

## Cross-Cutting Analysis

### The Three-Phase Progression is the Central Narrative

The summit's core message follows a clear arc:

1. **The Problem** (Slides 1-2): Human labor is hitting capacity limits, especially in APAC. Investment in AI is accelerating across all business functions.
2. **The Skills Shift** (Slide 3): AI literacy is the #1 skill, but human strengths (adaptability, conflict resolution, innovative thinking) remain critical.
3. **The Framework** (Slide 4): Microsoft's "Frontier Firm" model provides a 3-phase roadmap.
4. **Phase 1 in Action** (Slide 5): Generic Copilot use cases across 7 departments.
5. **Phase 2 in Action** (Slides 6, 7, 8): Role-specific scenarios with demos in quality data analysis and patent comparison.
6. **Phase 2→3 Transition** (Slide 9): The FAQ agent demo shows the shift from tool-assisted to agent-driven workflows with human oversight.
7. **Phase 3 Vision** (Slide 10): Fully autonomous AI systems operating across entire business processes.

### Key Implications for GTM Strategy

- **Taiwan paradox:** Only 47% of leaders feel productivity pressure, but 90% of workers feel overwhelmed — the highest in APAC. This gap represents a significant opportunity for AI adoption messaging.
- **Phase-based selling:** Organizations are at different phases; GTM should meet customers where they are rather than pushing Phase 3 to Phase 1 companies.
- **Human-in-the-loop is the bridge:** The FAQ demo (Slide 9) is the most practical example of how to transition from Phase 2 to Phase 3 — start with agent-drafted content, add human review, then gradually increase autonomy.
- **Cross-functional AI:** The investment data shows broad interest (not concentrated in IT), meaning GTM should target line-of-business leaders, not just CIOs.
